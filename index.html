From Our Bench

Made slowly, on purpose

Juriah Monique Adornments started at a kitchen table with a bead tray and too many half-finished necklaces. Every design still begins that way — strung bead by bead, held up to the light, and only kept if it feels like something you'd reach for on a soft, ordinary day.

We work in glass, silver, and hand-picked stone beads, in batches small enough that we still know each piece before it leaves the workbench.
